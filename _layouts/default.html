Yuzhe Gu

I am an ML researcher at UC Santa Cruz, advised by Prof. Yi Zhang.

I received my M.S. from the University of Pennsylvania, and my dual B.S. degrees from Duke Kunshan University and Duke University.

My current research focuses on robotics, with a particular interest in developing agentic systems for general-purpose humanoid robots in the real world, and reliably benchmarking their behaviors.

Previously, I also worked on problems in natural language processing and speech and dialogue processing, with a focus on improving the algorithmic efficiency and effectiveness through mathematical principles.
